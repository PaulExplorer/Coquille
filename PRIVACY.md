# Privacy Policy

Coquille is a Firefox and Chrome extension that corrects French spelling and
grammar directly inside the text fields of the pages you visit.

**Short version: everything happens on your device. The extension never sends
your text, or anything else, to any server.**

## What the extension does with your data

- The text you type is analyzed **locally**: the checking engine
  ([Grammalecte](https://grammalecte.net/)) is bundled inside the extension
  and runs in your browser's background context.
- No text is collected, logged, stored on a server, or transmitted over the
  network. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no remote code, and no
  tracking of any kind.
- Your settings (disabled sites, ignored words, ignored rules) are stored
  locally in the browser through the extension's `storage` permission and
  never leave your device.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Read and change content of any website** (`<all_urls>`): the extension
  underlines corrections directly in the page's text fields, on any site
  where people write in French. This is the same permission the original
  Grammalecte extension requires.
- **Storage** (`storage`): keeps your settings and ignored words in the
  browser.

## Site disable switch

You can disable the extension globally or per site from the popup or the
options page. Disabled means the extension literally does not analyze or
touch the page.
